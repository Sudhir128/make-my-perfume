https://www.pexels.com/search/scent/ for the existing products, temporarily,
until real Make My Perfume product photography exists.

text. Only photographs whose bottles carry **no** brand markings were used
(blank labels or unlabelled glass). Derived square crops live in
`src/assets/products/{slug}-{480,960}.webp`; mapping in
`src/data/productImages.ts`. Product facts were not changed.
